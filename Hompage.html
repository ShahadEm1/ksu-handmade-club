Painting Free

Painting Introductory Workshop

Discover the expressive world of acrylic painting on canvas. Participants will learn basic color mixing, brush techniques, and composition to create their own original artwork.

Includes all art supplies, canvas, and a take-home palette!

with Noura Al-Harbi 0 SAR
Crochet 30 SAR

Beginner Crochet Workshop

Learn the fundamentals of crochet from scratch. By the end of this 3-hour session you'll have completed your very own handmade granny square!

All yarn and ergonomic crochet hooks are provided for use during the session.

with Layan Al-Otaibi 30 SAR
Pottery 45 SAR

Pottery Wheel Introductory Class

Discover the meditative art of wheel-thrown pottery. Participants will center clay, open a form, and pull walls to shape their first bowl or cup. All materials and tools are provided.

Glazing and kiln firing for your best piece are included in the ticket price!

with Reem Al-Zahrani 45 SAR
