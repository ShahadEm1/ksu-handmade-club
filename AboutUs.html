About KSU Handmade Club

KSU Handmade Club is a university-centred platform dedicated to bringing King Saud University students and passionate hobbyists together around manual arts and craftsmanship — crochet, pottery, sewing, and more.

The club connects learners with skilled student instructors through organised workshops, classes, and creative experiences. Our positive mission is to foster creativity, support student wellbeing, and help relieve academic stress through hands-on artistic expression.

Beyond teaching practical artisan skills, the club actively empowers talented students by providing a digital exhibition showcase where they can display their handmade pieces, receive peer recognition through community voting, and gain real opportunities to feature and sell their crafts at dedicated university bazaars and campus exhibitions.
